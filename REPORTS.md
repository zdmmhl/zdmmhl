## Evidence boundaries

- Binary exploitation writeups and the [screenshot archive](https://github.com/zdmmhl/security-lab-screenshots) refer to controlled coursework labs. Some original proofs and draft scripts are incomplete.
- Research proposals are not deployed systems. Historical literature discussion is not current product or legal guidance.
- Machine-learning presentation metrics are saved results. Evaluation caveats, including possible leakage in the churn workflow, are explicit in the linked reports.
- Forensics is represented by [de-identified methodology notes](https://github.com/zdmmhl/digital-forensics-case-studies); original case reports contain identity and evidence material and remain local.
- Course specifications, blank report templates, private captures, contact records, datasets and model checkpoints are not represented as personal completed projects.
